SIMPLE PROCESS

Your journey, made simple.

From choosing your car to hitting the road, VELOX keeps the entire rental process simple.

01

Search & Choose Your Car

Browse available cars by location, dates, and vehicle type. Select the car that suits you and review the full details, price, and terms.

02

Book & Pay Online

Fill in your details, choose any extras (insurance, additional drivers,delivery etc.), and complete payment securely on our website. Once payment is successful, you will instantly receive a downloadable booking confirmation voucher (and in your account). Keep this voucher: a staff at velox will need to check the voucher when you arrive.

03

Prepare What You Need

Before pickup (or delivery), make sure you have the following ready:

  • Your booking confirmation voucher (digital or printed)
  • A valid physical driver’s licence (original only — photocopies or photos are not accepted)
  • A valid passport or government-issued photo ID
  • A physical credit or debit card in the main driver’s name (this is required for the security deposit, even if you already paid for the rental online)
  • An International Driving Permit (IDP) if you are renting outside your home country or your licence is not in the Roman alphabet (check the rental terms for your location)
04

Collect Your Car

You have two options (depending on availability at your location):

  • Go to the pickup point shown on your voucher (airport desk, city branch, or shuttle location). Present the documents listed above. Our team will verify everything, place a security deposit hold on your card, inspect the car with you, and hand over the keys.
  • Delivery to your location (where available) We can deliver the car to your home, hotel, or office for an additional fee. You still need to present the same documents at the point of delivery.
05

Drive & Return

Enjoy your rental. When you’re finished, return the car to the agreed drop-off location (or arrange collection if delivery was used). The security deposit will be released after the car is inspected, provided there is no damage or outstanding charges.
